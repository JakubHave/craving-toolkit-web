## Citation Strategy for Our Ebook
Best approach:
- keep in-text references light and human
- include a final “Further Reading / References” section
- mention when an exercise comes from or is inspired by SMART Recovery, motivational interviewing, or mindfulness-based craving work
- avoid fake academic citation style unless we fully source every claim
