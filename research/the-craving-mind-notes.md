# The Craving Mind — Judson Brewer — Reading Notes

_Date: 2026-03-11_

## Why this book matters for our ebook
Judson Brewer's *The Craving Mind* gives us one of the most practical, moment-to-moment tools for the ebook: mindfulness-based craving intervention.

This complements Lembke and Lewis perfectly:
- Lembke tells us **why** our reward systems get out of balance
- Lewis tells us **how** addiction becomes learned
- Brewer tells us **what to do** in the moment when a craving hits

---

## Core concepts

### 1. Habit loops
Every craving and addictive behavior follows a pattern:
- Cue → Craving → Routine → Reward

Understanding this loop is the first step to interrupting it.

### 2. Awareness beats willpower
Willpower tries to suppress urges (exhausting, often fails).
Awareness investigates urges (sustainable, builds capacity).

### 3. Curiosity disarms cravings
Instead of fighting or giving in, get curious:
- What does this craving feel like in my body?
- How intense is it on a scale of 0-10?
- Where does it start? Where does it go?
- What is this trying to get me?

This approach weakens the automaticity of the loop.

### 4. Urge surfing
Already in our book from SMART Recovery. Brewer gives it more neuroscience and depth.

## Best practical tools for our ebook

### 1. Habit loop mapping exercise
- Cue (what triggered it?)
- Urge (what did it feel like?)
- Routine (what did you do?)
- Reward (what did it give you in the short term?)
- Consequences (what came after?)

### 2. Curiosity micropractice (60-90 seconds)
- Pause and name the urge
- Get curious: where in the body? What quality? How intense?
- Stay with it for 60-120 seconds without acting
- Notice how it changes

### 3. Body scan for cravings
- Scan from feet to head
- Label sensations
- Breathe into tight areas

### 4. Alternative reward planning
- List 3 low-friction healthy replacements
- Use after curiosity practice if you choose not to act

## Useful phrases
- "I'm curious: what is this urge like right now?"
- "Can I stay with this for 60 seconds and just watch?"
- "Notice the wave: rise, peak, fall."
- "This is temporary. It will change."

## Cautions
- Not a substitute for medical detox
- Can surface intense emotions - teach grounding first for trauma
- Avoid shaming - curiosity must be non-judgmental
- For severe SUDs, combine with professional support
