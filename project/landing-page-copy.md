What people need is something practical:
- what to do in the first 5 minutes
- how to interrupt the routine before relapse happens
- how to handle stress, loneliness, shame, boredom, and self-sabotage
- how to recover quickly after a slip instead of giving up completely

That’s what this guide is for.

## FAQ
### Is this therapy or medical treatment?
No. This is educational material based on lived experience, recovery insight, and practical tools. It is not a substitute for therapy, treatment, or medical care.

### Is this only for drug addiction?
No. The material is designed around craving patterns that can show up across alcohol, drugs, overeating, and other compulsive behaviors.

### Is this for people early in recovery only?
No. It may also help people who have had periods of recovery but still struggle with certain triggers, slips, or relapse cycles.

### Will this cure addiction?
No. No honest person should promise that. The goal is to provide useful tools that may help you manage cravings and stay grounded in recovery.
