# Product Outline

## Working Title
Craving Toolkit

## Subtitle Options
- Practical tools to fight urges, stop spirals, and stay in recovery
- A practical guide for managing addiction cravings in real life
- Fast, grounded tools for the hardest moments

## Format
- Short ebook / PDF
- Target length: 20–40 pages
- Later: audiobook edition

## Goal of the Book
Help people move from panic / autopilot / relapse momentum into awareness, interruption, and action.

## Suggested Table of Contents

### 1. Introduction: This is for the moment when it gets hard
- why cravings feel bigger than logic
- what this guide is and is not
- how to use the guide in real life

### 2. What a craving really is
- urge vs decision
- craving wave: rise, peak, fall
- body, stress, memory, ritual, environment
- why “just use willpower” often fails

### 3. The first 5 minutes: emergency tools
- pause script
- urge surfing
- delay for 10 minutes
- change the room / location immediately
- cold water / breathing / walk / body reset
- text or call someone
- remove access to the substance / behavior
- do not negotiate with the craving

### 4. The first 30 minutes: how to break the spiral
- food, sleep, stress, loneliness check
- interrupt the ritual chain
- replace secrecy with contact
- structured distraction
- move from emotion to action
- use a written recovery card

### 5. Tools by trigger type
- stress and overwhelm
- loneliness and emptiness
- boredom and restlessness
- anger and resentment
- celebration / reward mode
- shame after a mistake
- social pressure

### 6. Different addiction patterns
- alcohol
- drugs
- overeating / binge patterns
- compulsive habits / process addictions

Note: emphasize patterns and mechanisms rather than pretending all addictions are identical.

### 7. Different personality styles in addiction
- the all-or-nothing person
- the secretive isolator
- the rebel / self-saboteur
- the high-performer who hides pain
- the comfort-seeker / emotional soother

For each type:
- common thinking traps
- typical relapse setup
- tools that may help most

### 8. What to do after a slip
- do not turn one slip into a collapse
- stop the shame spiral
- tell the truth fast
- review the chain without self-hatred
- reset the next hour, not just “someday”

### 9. Daily practices that reduce cravings over time
- sleep
- routine
- food / blood sugar stability
- movement
- support and honesty
- reducing trigger exposure
- building identity beyond addiction

### 10. Build your personal anti-craving plan
- top triggers
- warning signs
- emergency actions
- who to contact
- what to remove / block / avoid
- what to do in the next hour

### 11. Final words
- progress over perfection
- recovery is messy but possible
- ask for real help when needed

## Bonus Materials
- one-page craving emergency card
- trigger map worksheet
- relapse-chain worksheet
- 7-day stabilization checklist

## Product Tiers
### Tier 1
PDF only

### Tier 2
PDF + audiobook

### Tier 3 (later)
Bundle with worksheets / printable tools

## Writing Principles
- short sections
- practical examples
- minimal jargon
- concrete steps
- honest tone
- zero preachiness
- avoid overpromising
