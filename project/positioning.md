# Product Positioning

## Core Idea
A paid practical recovery guide for people dealing with addiction cravings, based on lived experience, treatment exposure, and long-term sobriety.

## Working Promise
Practical tools to get through cravings, interrupt compulsive patterns, and reduce the chance of relapse.

## Best Initial Positioning
**A practical craving-management toolkit for people struggling with addiction and compulsive behaviors.**

This should be framed as:
- experience-based
- practical
- compassionate
- non-preachy
- not a substitute for professional care

## Credibility Angle
Jakub is not just repackaging generic wellness advice. He brings:
- 10+ years clean after struggles with overeating, alcohol, and drugs
- lived experience with cravings, relapse dynamics, shame, and recovery work
- treatment / rehab exposure
- serious reading and learning on addiction
- software-builder mindset that can turn ideas into usable tools

## Audience
Primary audience:
- people actively struggling with cravings
- people early in recovery
- people trying to quit or reduce harmful addictive behaviors
- people who want practical tools, not theory-heavy material

Secondary audience:
- family/friends who want to understand craving management
- people who cannot yet access formal support but want something useful now

## Pain Points
- cravings feel overpowering and urgent
- relapse often happens in a short emotional window
- most advice is too vague: “just stay strong”
- addiction can show up differently across substances and compulsive behaviors
- shame makes people isolate and stop using support

## Message
This product helps people:
- recognize what cravings actually are
- survive the high-risk window
- use practical interventions in real time
- build routines that reduce craving intensity over time
- recover faster after slips instead of collapsing into self-hate

## Positioning Statement
For people struggling with addiction cravings or compulsive behaviors, this guide offers practical, field-tested tools to manage urges in the moment and stay grounded in recovery. It is based on lived experience, treatment insight, and years of sobriety — not empty motivation.

## Tone
- direct
- calm
- compassionate
- grounded
- not clinical unless useful
- not hypey
- not morally judgmental

## Important Boundaries
Avoid claims like:
- cure addiction
- guaranteed sobriety
- replace therapy or treatment
- works for everyone

Prefer language like:
- practical support
- educational material
- lived experience
- tools that may help
- not medical advice

## Simple Offer Structure
- Main offer: PDF / ebook
- Optional add-on: audiobook
- Optional later upsell: worksheets, trackers, emergency craving card

## Suggested Disclaimer
This guide is educational and based on lived experience, reading, and recovery insight. It is not medical advice and is not a substitute for professional treatment, therapy, or emergency support.
