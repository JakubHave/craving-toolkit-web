## Best Current Picks

   - clear
   - practical
   - easy to understand
   - broad enough for ebook + audio + worksheets

2. **Stop the Spiral**
   - emotionally strong
   - memorable
   - good landing-page headline material

3. **When the Urge Hits**
   - human
   - relatable
   - good for audience resonance

## Recommended Direction
Use a two-part naming system:

**Brand / product name:** Craving Toolkit  
**Subtitle:** Practical tools to fight urges, stop spirals, and stay in recovery

Alternative:

**Brand / product name:** Stop the Spiral  
**Subtitle:** A practical guide to managing addiction cravings in real life

## Domain / Branding Thoughts
Best to keep the domain broad enough to allow future products. Avoid going too niche or too clinical.

Potential brand directions:
- cravingtoolkit.com
- stopthespiral.co
- urgehelp.com
- recoverytoolkit.co
- backtoground.co

If the exact .com is unavailable, .co or .io can work, but a clean .com is still nicest for trust if available.
